# Quality and verification

This release is completing 20 development experiments under [rubric v1](../quality/rubric.json). [The public experiment records](../quality/experiments.json) contain executed results and explicit limits. Final production and clean-checkout results will be recorded before publication.

The initial fixed desktop suite passed 5 of 16 cases. Supplementary probes were tested before and after their candidate changes. These are implementation-agent acceptance assessments, not independent human, actual-phone, performance, or market ratings.
